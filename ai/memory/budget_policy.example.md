# Budget Policy (example)

Rules that guide how this household runs its envelope budget. The AI analyst
uses these when suggesting adjustments or building a "get back on track" plan.
Copy this file to `budget_policy.md`, remove "example" from your thinking, and
edit every line to match your home.

## Methodology

Zero-based budgeting via Actual Budget. Every dollar of income is assigned to
a category before it can be spent. Unspent money rolls over inside the same
envelope (Actual's carry-over setting enabled per category).

## Priority order for covering shortfalls

1. Pause dining-out and entertainment first: instant lever, no impact on commitments
2. Pull from the Miscellaneous buffer before touching any other envelope
3. Groceries can flex about 15 percent in a crunch month but should be restored the next month
4. Subscriptions: cut anything over $10/mo that has not been used in 30 days
5. Never raid: Emergency Fund, Insurance premiums, Rent or Mortgage, Minimum debt payments

## Emergency fund target

3 to 6 months of essential expenses (housing + utilities + groceries + insurance).
Current target: $FILL_IN. Track progress in goals.json.

## Debt payoff strategy

Avalanche: extra payments go to the highest-interest debt first.
See debts.json for current balances and rates.

## "Get back on track" trigger

Any month that ends with the total credit card carry balance increasing versus
the prior month. Action: schedule a budget review session within 3 days.

## Savings targets (monthly)

- Emergency fund build: $FILL_IN/mo until fully funded, then maintain
- Retirement: maximize any employer match first
- Kids' activities: $FILL_IN per child per month
- Vacation and travel: $FILL_IN/mo into a dedicated envelope

## Notes

- Bank and card transactions sync into Actual daily; review uncategorized
  transactions at least twice per week.
- Fixed monthly income arrives on the first business day of the month; assign
  it immediately.
