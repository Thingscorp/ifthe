# Major Financial Decisions Log (example)

One entry per major decision, newest first. The AI analyst drafts entries;
a human approves and keeps the file. Copy to `decisions.md` to start yours.

## Template

### YYYY-MM  Decision title

- **Decision:** what was decided, in one line.
- **Why:** the reason, with the numbers that mattered.
- **Revisit:** when to look at it again, if ever.

### 2026-01  Example: switched the emergency fund to a high-yield savings account

- **Decision:** Moved the emergency fund to a high-yield savings account at the same bank.
- **Why:** Same access, better interest, no new institution to track.
- **Revisit:** If the rate drops below the checking account's, move it back.
