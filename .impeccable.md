## Design Context

Written by the impeccable teach flow (skill-chain run, 2026-11-20). Context comes from the owner's recorded rulings and the project record, not from inference off the code.

### Users

Households running their own services, and the builders who would copy this. They arrive from a link (a gadget showcase, a repo, a friend's kitchen counter) and give the page under a minute. The job: understand what ifthe is, believe it is real, and reach the code.

### Brand Personality

Plain, specific, honest about status. Three words: factual, spare, Pebble-minded. The emotional goal is recognition, not excitement: this is the page equivalent of the product, one screen with the day on it.

### Aesthetic Direction

repebble.com's UI is the model, by owner ruling, verified from its own stylesheet in the REA run at ~/workspace/rea-repebble/REA.md. White page, one centered 1100px column, Inter, hairline dividers, one accent (muted sage #5c6650), ink #1c1a17, product lineup cards, plain intro paragraphs with bolded traits, resource link columns, minimal footer. Anti-references: SaaS landing templates, hero headlines, gradient anything, dark mode, feature walls, fake social proof, mood adjectives in copy. The words "quiet" and "calm" are banned in ifthe copy, and em-dashes are banned everywhere.

### Design Principles

1. The page is a spec sheet written by a person: facts, sizes, and honest status over adjectives.
2. One accent, one font, hairlines instead of boxes. Nothing decorative that is not information.
3. Every claim on the page resolves today: links work, statuses are true, the code is one click away.
4. Match the model exactly where the model is measured (spacing, type sizes, divider placement); adapt only where the brand differs (accent color, wordmark, content).
5. The page works with scripts disabled and prints clean. The product is e-paper; the page should feel like it could be printed on it.
